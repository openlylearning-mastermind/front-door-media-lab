Questions contractors ask

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Most videos show your crew and the work. If you like talking to the camera, we'll use that too.

Will this get me more calls?

We can't promise a number, and you should be wary of anyone who does. We test different openings every month and show you which videos people watched and clicked, so we keep what works and drop what doesn't.

Do you post the videos for us?

We write the captions and hand you every video sized for each platform. You or your office posts them. If you want us to post too, ask on the call.

What area do you cover?

We film on site across West Michigan. If you're farther out, ask and we'll tell you honestly whether it works.

What if a job site isn't ready on shoot day?

We reschedule at no charge. Weather and job sites in Michigan don't keep to a calendar, and we plan for that.

Thirty minutes on the phone. Tell us how you get work today and we'll tell you honestly whether video would help.
